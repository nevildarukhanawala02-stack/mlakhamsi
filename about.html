Our Story

In 1933, business began in Bombay.
It never stopped growing.

Business began as a trading venture in Bombay — procuring groundnuts from local farmers and finding buyers wherever the crop was needed. No shortcuts. No unnecessary middlemen. Just a product, a price, and a promise to deliver.

Over the following decades, that promise took us to 75 countries. We added sesame seeds, castor oil, spices, and pulses — always processing in-house, always building direct relationships. Along the way, we became a Star Export House, listed on the BSE, and built one of the few Indian agri-facilities with laser aflatoxin sorting technology.

More than 9 decades later, the business is larger. The supply chain is more complex. The promise is the same: you can verify everything we do.

What Drives Us

Three principles. Ninety-plus years. No exceptions.

01

Proof Over Claim

We do not ask buyers to trust us. We give them the documentation to verify us. Lab reports, certificates, audited financials — everything is available without a phone call. If we cannot prove it, we do not say it.

02

Direct Relationships

From the farmer in Gujarat to the food manufacturer in Rotterdam — we maintain direct relationships at every point in the supply chain. No unnecessary intermediaries. No opacity. The same model we started with in 1933.

03

Supply Continuity

Our pan-India procurement network and multiple port access means that when one region faces a difficult harvest, we source from another. Supply continuity is not accidental — it is built into how we operate. 90+ years of consistent delivery is the proof.
